Refund Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

We want you to be happy with Dockling Pro. Try it free for 7 days in the app before you buy: no card, and nothing is charged when the trial ends.

Full refund within 14 days

If Dockling Pro isn't right for you, email 694561981@qq.com within 14 days of purchase with your order email or license key, and we'll issue a full refund. No questions needed.

How refunds are made

Refunds are processed by Creem, our merchant of record, back to your original payment method. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Once refunded, the license key is disabled. Dockling goes back to the free version at its next online check (at most once a day); Pro widgets already on your shelf are not removed, they just show a lock.

After 14 days

Purchases older than 14 days are generally not refundable, but please contact us if something is wrong and we'll do our best to help.

Gift codes

Gift codes (starting with DKG) were not paid for, so there is nothing to refund.
