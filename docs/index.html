码头（Dockling）是免费、开源的 macOS 程序坞扩展栏：在系统程序坞旁边摆上实时小组件、应用分组和文件夹，也可以直接用它替代程序坞。界面全部是简体中文。

Dockling (码头) is a free, open-source Dock shelf for macOS. Put live widgets, app groups and folders right next to the system Dock, or use it in place of the Dock. The interface is in Simplified Chinese.

首次打开First launch

码头使用 ad-hoc 签名：没有 Apple 开发者证书，也没有经过公证。下载的文件本身没有问题，只是首次打开时会被门禁（Gatekeeper）拦下。只需要处理一次。

Dockling is ad-hoc signed, with no Apple Developer certificate and no notarization. The download is fine, but Gatekeeper stops it the first time. You only need to do this once.

  1. 打开 .dmg，把 码头 拖到其中的“应用程序”替身上。（也可以使用 .zip，解压后自行移到“应用程序”文件夹。）请先完成安装再进行下一步，因为接下来授予的权限与应用所在位置绑定。
  2. 双击打开。macOS 会提示无法验证该应用不含恶意软件，点按 完成。
  3. 打开 系统设置 ▸ 隐私与安全性，滚动到 安全性 部分，会看到码头已被阻止的提示以及 仍要打开 按钮。点按它并验证身份。
  4. 再次打开码头，并确认 仍要打开。
  1. Open the .dmg and drag 码头 (Dockling) onto the Applications alias inside it. (Or use the .zip and move the app into Applications yourself.) Install it first: the permissions you grant later are tied to where the app lives.
  2. Double-click it. macOS says it cannot verify the app is free of malware; click Done.
  3. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and you will see that Dockling was blocked, with an Open Anyway button. Click it and authenticate.
  4. Open Dockling again and confirm Open Anyway.

如果 macOS 仍然拒绝打开，或提示应用“已损坏”，请在“终端”中移除下载文件的隔离属性：If macOS still refuses, or says the app is damaged, remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dockling.app

启动后请看菜单栏：码头是辅助型应用，没有程序坞图标，也不会打开窗口。如果双击后似乎什么都没发生，其实它已经正常启动了。After launch, look in the menu bar: Dockling is an agent app with no Dock icon and no window. If nothing seems to happen, it is already running.

常见问题FAQ

码头免费吗？Is Dockling free?

免费，而且开源（MIT 许可）。所有功能都免费；赞助者只额外解锁专属配色、表盘和喝水角色这类外观，码头不联网验证，全凭诚信。

Yes, and open source under the MIT License. Every feature is free. Supporters only get cosmetic extras such as shelf colours, clock faces and water characters, unlocked on the honour system with no online check.

为什么打开时提示“无法验证”？Why does macOS say it cannot verify the app?

码头使用 ad-hoc 签名：没有 Apple 开发者证书，也没有经过公证，所以首次打开时会被门禁（Gatekeeper）拦下。下载的文件本身没有问题，源码全部公开，也可以自己构建。处理方法见首次打开。

Dockling is ad-hoc signed, with no Apple Developer certificate and no notarization, so Gatekeeper stops it the first time. The download itself is fine, the source is public, and you can build it yourself. See First launch.

为什么要授予这些权限？Why does it ask for permissions?

只在用到时才请求，不用的小组件不需要：自动化（正在播放、邮件）、日历和提醒事项（打开对应面板时）、麦克风（录音）、录音权限（应用音量）、完全磁盘访问权限（废纸篓大小）、辅助功能（剪贴板点按即粘贴）、通知（久坐和喝水提醒）。

Only when a widget needs it, and never for widgets you do not use: Automation (Now Playing, Mail), Calendars and Reminders (when you open those panels), Microphone (Recorder), audio recording (Per-app volume), Full Disk Access (Trash size), Accessibility (paste on click from Clipboard) and Notifications (stand-up and water reminders).

我的数据去哪了？Where does my data go?

都保存在你的 Mac 本地，没有账号，也不收集统计。只有少数小组件需要联网：天气（MET Norway、Open-Meteo）、汇率、加密货币（CoinGecko）、股票、GitHub、Codex 用量（只发给 chatgpt.com）、收音机，以及假期安排更新和检查更新。翻译使用 macOS 本机翻译，文字不离开这台 Mac。

It stays on your Mac. There is no account and no analytics. Only a few widgets go online: weather (MET Norway, Open-Meteo), currency, crypto (CoinGecko), stocks, GitHub, Codex usage (sent to chatgpt.com only) and radio, plus holiday-schedule updates and update checks. Translation uses on-device macOS translation, so your text never leaves the Mac.

码头和 Dockset 是什么关系？How is Dockling related to Dockset?

没有关系。码头是开源项目 Docket（nparashar150，MIT）的非官方简体中文本地化和扩展分支；Docket 的灵感来自独立开发者的付费应用 Dockset。码头与 Dockset 及上游 Docket 均无关联，也未获得其认可。如果你想要打磨完善、有售后支持的正式产品，请购买 Dockset。

None. Dockling is an unofficial Simplified Chinese localization and extension of the open-source Docket (by nparashar150, MIT), which was itself inspired by Dockset, a paid app by an independent developer. Dockling is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dockset or upstream Docket. If you want a polished, supported product, buy Dockset.

怎么反馈问题或建议？How do I report a bug or suggest something?

请到 GitHub 提交 Issue，中文或英文都可以。附上 macOS 版本、码头版本和截图会很有帮助。

Open an issue on GitHub, in Chinese or English. Your macOS version, Dockling version and a screenshot help a lot.
