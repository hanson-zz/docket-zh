程序坞自定义应用图标Custom app icons in the Dock Pro 1.0 新增New in 1.0

给程序坞里的 App 换一个自己喜欢的图标。只改码头里显示的样子，不动系统，也不动 App 本身；点按启动、运行指示和角标都照旧。

Give any app in the Dock an icon you like. Only what Dockling draws changes: macOS and the app itself are left alone, and launching, the running dot and badges work as before.

  • 右键换：右键 App 选「更换图标…」，挑一张图片（PNG、JPG、HEIC、ICNS，自动缩放成方形），或选另一个 App 用它的图标。想改回来就点「恢复默认图标」。Right-click: choose Change Icon… on an app, then pick an image (PNG, JPG, HEIC or ICNS, scaled to a square) or another app to borrow its icon. Restore Default Icon puts the original back.
  • 按住 Option 拖上去：把图片或另一个 App 拖到扩展栏或分组窗口里的 App 图标上，按住 Option 松手就换好。不按 Option 拖文件，照旧是用这个 App 打开。Hold Option and drop: drag an image or another app onto an app icon on the shelf or in a group window, hold Option and let go. Without Option, dropping a file still opens it in that app.
  • 码头自己的图标：新增 7 款小熊猫配色，深色、万圣节、圣诞节免费，戴皇冠的蓝、红、金、炫彩 4 款属于 Pro，在 设置 ▸ 通用 ▸ 应用图标 里换。Dockling's own icon: 7 new red panda colours. Dark, Halloween and Christmas are free; the four crowned ones (blue, red, gold, rainbow) are Pro. Pick one in Settings ▸ General ▸ App Icon.

输入法自动切换Automatic input source switching 含 ProSome Pro 1.0 新增New in 1.0

切到哪个 App，就换成它的输入法；刘海下落下一个液态玻璃小胶囊，告诉你换成了哪个。

Switch to an app and its input source comes with it, while a small Liquid Glass capsule drops from the notch to show which one.

  • 免费：自动切换；没有规则的 App 用默认输入法，或按 App 记住上次用的；提示放在刘海下、光标旁或关掉，停留时间可调。Free: automatic switching; apps without a rule get your default input source or the one they used last; the hint sits below the notch, beside the cursor or off, for as long as you like.
  • 按 App 规则 Pro：一个 App 一条规则，比如终端固定 ABC、备忘录固定拼音。Per-app rules Pro: one rule per app, such as ABC for Terminal and Pinyin for Notes.
  • 读取和切换输入法不需要权限，只有「光标旁」要辅助功能权限。Reading and switching input sources needs no permission; only Beside the Cursor needs Accessibility.

A card on the shelf lists your running apps; click one to switch to it. Its panel is a ⌘Tab-style grid of big icons: pick an app to see its windows as thumbnails below, then click one to jump straight to it.

  • 面板：固定和正在运行的 App 排成大图标网格，列数跟着面板宽度变，开了多个窗口的在右上角标出窗口数；方向键移动，回车切换，空格展开，Esc 收起。The panel: pinned and running apps in a grid of big icons whose columns follow the panel width, with a window count on apps that have more than one; arrow keys move, Return switches, Space expands and Esc closes.
  • 窗口缩略图：点缩略图跳到那个窗口，「切到前台」只切 App；缩略图需要屏幕录制权限，没给时列出窗口标题（读窗口需要辅助功能权限）。Window thumbnails: click one to jump to that window, or use Bring to Front to switch to the app only; thumbnails need Screen Recording, and without it you get window titles (reading windows needs Accessibility).

Dockling only does what your widgets need. We have not measured CPU or memory, so there are no numbers here, just what the code does: online widgets such as Stocks, Watchlist and Crypto fetch data only once they are on the shelf, and refresh on a 5-minute timer; System Activity, Network and Battery sample this Mac once a second without going online; and when the shelf auto-hides it reads the pointer position about 30 times a second to know when to slide out, with no keyboard or mouse monitoring permission, and not at all when auto-hide is off. 系统程序坞自动隐藏、放在左右两侧或开着全屏应用时会怎样？What about a hidden Dock, a Dock on the side or full-screen apps?

By default Match Apple's Dock is on in Settings: the shelf follows the Dock's size, magnification and auto-hide, and takes the other edge on its own, on the left when the Dock is at the bottom and at the bottom when the Dock is on the left or right, so the two never stack. 会改动系统程序坞吗？卸载后能恢复吗？Does it change Apple's Dock? Can I undo it?

默认的“程序坞和扩展栏”不改系统程序坞，只读取它的设置。只有两种情况会写入系统程序坞的设置（com.apple.dock）：一是你在“布局”里把存好的程序坞布局“应用到程序坞”或用快捷键、专注模式切换，这只改程序坞里的应用列表（persistent-apps），不碰右边的文件夹和最近使用，码头会先存一份你最初的程序坞，菜单栏布局菜单里的“恢复原始程序坞”和 设置 ▸ 布局 里的“恢复原始状态”都能改回去；退出码头时应用列表不会自动改回，卸载前请先点一次恢复。二是选“仅扩展栏”，第一次会弹窗征求同意，然后把程序坞移到扩展栏那条边、关掉自动隐藏、调整图标大小再用扩展栏盖住；改之前会记下原来的自动隐藏、大小和位置，切回别的模式或退出码头时改回去，万一码头意外退出，下次启动时补上。

The default Dock and Shelf setup never changes Apple's Dock; it only reads its settings. Dockling writes the Dock's settings (com.apple.dock) in exactly two cases. One: you apply a saved Dock layout from Layouts with Apply to Dock, a shortcut or a Focus mode. That changes only the Dock's app list (persistent-apps), never the folders and recents on the right, and Dockling first keeps a copy of your original Dock, which Restore Original Dock in the menu bar's layout menu or Restore Original in Settings ▸ Layouts puts back. Quitting does not put the app list back, so restore it before you uninstall. Two: you choose Shelf only. Dockling asks first, then moves the Dock to the shelf's edge, turns its auto-hide off and resizes it so the shelf can cover it. It records your auto-hide, size and edge before changing them and restores them when you switch to another setup or quit; if Dockling ever quits unexpectedly, it restores them at the next launch.
